Frequently Asked Questions

The scan result doesn't look accurate. What should I do?

AI-generated results are estimates based on the tongue photo you submit and are for general wellness reference only — not medical diagnosis. For best results, use good lighting, stick out your tongue naturally, fill the on-screen oval guide, and hold still. If results consistently seem off, email us with a description and we'll investigate.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete My Data inside the App to remove on-device scan history, locally stored tongue photos, and profile information. For any server-side data removal requests, email us and we will process it within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is my tongue photo stored or shared?

Tongue photos are sent over HTTPS to our servers solely to generate your wellness analysis, then removed from our primary processing flow after the scan completes. A copy may be saved on your device so you can view history. We do not sell or share your photos with data brokers. See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. To cancel, change plans, or request a refund, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. You can also tap Restore Purchase in the App's Settings if you reinstalled on a new device.

The app crashes or won't load. What should I try?

First, force-quit and reopen the App. If that doesn't help, check that your iOS is up to date, then try deleting and reinstalling. If the problem continues, email us at liveglowapp@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.

Is Tongue Ritual a medical app?

No. Tongue Ritual is a wellness and educational tool rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine observation frameworks. Outputs are AI-generated estimates and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider or licensed TCM practitioner for health concerns.
