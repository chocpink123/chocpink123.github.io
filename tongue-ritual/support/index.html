Frequently Asked Questions

The photo observation doesn't look accurate. What should I do?

AI-generated descriptions are based only on visible features in the tongue photo you submit. They may be incomplete or incorrect and are for general wellness education only, not medical diagnosis. For a more consistent photo, use indirect natural light, fill the on-screen oval guide, and hold still. If descriptions consistently seem off, email us and we'll investigate.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete My Data inside the App at any time to remove on-device scan history, locally stored tongue photos, profile information, preferences, and usage data. Server-side analysis data is removed through scheduled cleanup after processing. For any additional server-side data removal requests, email us and we will process them within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is my tongue photo stored or shared?

Before sending a photo, the App asks for permission and explains that the photo is sent through our server to Google LLC's Gemini API to generate the visual observation you requested. You can withdraw permission in Settings → Share Data with AI. A copy may be saved on your device so you can view history. We do not sell photos or share them with advertisers or data brokers. See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. To cancel, change plans, or request a refund, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. You can also tap Restore Purchase in the App's Settings if you reinstalled on a new device.

The app crashes or won't load. What should I try?

First, force-quit and reopen the App. If that doesn't help, check that your iOS is up to date, then try deleting and reinstalling. If the problem continues, email us at liveglowapp@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.

Is Tongue Ritual a medical app?

No. Tongue Ritual is a general wellness and educational photo journal inspired by Traditional Chinese Medicine observation frameworks. It describes visible photo features and does not measure health, diagnose conditions, or recommend treatment. Seek a doctor's advice in addition to using the App and before making medical decisions.
