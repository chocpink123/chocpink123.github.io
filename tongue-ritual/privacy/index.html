Tongue Ritual — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tongue Ritual ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes information when you use the Tongue Ritual: TCM Scan mobile application for iOS (the "App"). It also describes your choices and rights.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

This policy is designed to work alongside Apple's platform terms and your device settings. It does not describe third-party services you may access through links inside the App (for example, websites opened in Safari), which are governed by their own policies.

Summary

The App helps you capture tongue photos you choose to submit, generates Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM)–inspired wellness insights (such as balance scores, six signal readings, body patterns, food picks, and wellness tips), and stores certain results and profile details on your device.

When you run a tongue scan, your photo and related inputs are transmitted over HTTPS to servers operated by us or our service providers to generate results. You can delete on-device App data using Settings → Delete My Data. You can contact us about privacy using the email address at the end of this policy.

The App is not a medical device. Outputs are for general wellness and educational purposes only.

The data controller for personal data processed in connection with the App is the legal entity identified as the developer or seller of the App on the Apple App Store for your region ("Publisher"). If you have questions about which company is the Publisher, refer to the App Store product page or your purchase receipt.

For privacy requests, contact us at liveglowapp@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Content you provide. When you choose to capture a tongue photo with the in-app camera (or, if available, import a photo from your library) and request analysis, we process that image and any related inputs needed to run the feature.
  • Profile and preferences. Information you provide during onboarding or in Settings (such as display name, age range, health goals, daily habits, and symptom selections) may be stored on your device to personalize greetings and your experience.
  • Outputs and history. The App stores analysis outputs on your device so you can view results, trends, and history. This may include overall balance scores, status labels (for example, "Balanced" or "Damp"), six signal metrics (color, coating, moisture, shape, tip, and veins), body pattern summaries, wellness tips, food recommendations, timestamps, and optionally a blurred copy of your captured tongue photo associated with each reading.
  • Technical and usage data. We may process limited technical information needed to operate the App and secure our services, such as device type, operating system version, app version, language/locale, diagnostic logs, and error reports.
  • Subscriptions and purchases. If you purchase a subscription or other paid feature, Apple processes payment. We may receive subscription status, transaction identifiers, or similar information from Apple or our entitlement systems to unlock features.

Tongue Photographs

This section describes photographs you choose to capture or import when you use the tongue scan feature. This is not Face ID, TrueDepth identity data, or biometric enrollment templates created by iOS for device authentication.

What we collect: A tongue photo per scan you initiate, encoded for transport as JPEG (typically resized on-device before upload).

How we use it: Solely to generate in-app TCM-inspired wellness insights (scores, signal labels, body patterns, tips, and similar outputs), to show scan history and weekly balance trends on your device, and to operate, secure, and debug the analysis pipeline.

Third parties and storage: The image is sent over HTTPS to servers we operate, then forwarded to Google LLC's Gemini API for automated vision analysis. Google processes the image only to return the analysis output to our service; Google's processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy documentation applicable to API use. We do not sell tongue photos and do not share them with data brokers.

Retention: On-device scan history, scores, and related data (including locally saved tongue photo files linked to readings) remain until you delete app data (Settings → Delete My Data), uninstall the App, or erase the device. On our servers, the uploaded image is removed from our primary processing flow once analysis completes. Residual server logs or backups may briefly retain fragments consistent with our infrastructure practices.

AI Analysis and Automated Outputs

Certain features use automated systems, including machine learning models, to generate estimates, classifications, pattern names, and explanatory text based on your tongue photo. For cloud-based tongue scans, submitted images are analyzed using Google LLC's Gemini API (vision-capable model) via our backend; only outputs needed to display your results are returned to the App.

Outputs may be imperfect, incomplete, or incorrect. Automated results are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and do not constitute a TCM clinical consultation.
