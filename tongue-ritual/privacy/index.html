Tongue Ritual — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tongue Ritual ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes information when you use the Tongue Ritual: TCM Scan mobile application for iOS (the "App"). It also describes your choices and rights.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

This policy is designed to work alongside Apple's platform terms and your device settings. It does not describe third-party services you may access through links inside the App (for example, websites opened in Safari), which are governed by their own policies.

Summary

The App helps you capture tongue photos you choose to submit, generates TCM-inspired general wellness content (including a visual balance score, six feature cards, a body-pattern summary, food picks, and wellness tips), and stores certain results and profile details on your device.

Before the first AI observation, the App explains that your tongue photo will be sent through our server to Google LLC's Gemini API and asks for your permission. If you decline, the photo is not sent. You can withdraw permission at any time from Settings → Share Data with AI.

The App is not a medical device. Outputs are for general wellness and educational purposes only.

The data controller for personal data processed in connection with the App is the legal entity identified as the developer or seller of the App on the Apple App Store for your region ("Publisher"). If you have questions about which company is the Publisher, refer to the App Store product page or your purchase receipt.

For privacy requests, contact us at liveglowapp@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Content you provide. When you explicitly capture a tongue photo with the in-app camera or select one with Apple's photo picker and request an AI observation, we process that image. Your name, age range, onboarding answers, and saved history are not included in the AI request.
  • Profile and preferences. Information you provide during onboarding or in Settings (such as display name, age range, health goals, daily habits, and symptom selections) may be stored on your device to personalize greetings and your experience.
  • Outputs and history. The App stores results on your device so you can view trends and history. This may include a visual balance score, status labels, six feature cards, a TCM-inspired body-pattern summary, general wellness tips, food suggestions, timestamps, and a locally saved JPEG copy of your captured tongue photo.
  • Technical and usage data. We may process limited technical information needed to operate the App and secure our services, such as device type, operating system version, app version, language/locale, diagnostic logs, and error reports.
  • Subscriptions and purchases. If you purchase a subscription or other paid feature, Apple processes payment. We may receive subscription status, transaction identifiers, or similar information from Apple or our entitlement systems to unlock features.

Tongue Photographs

This section describes photographs you choose to capture or import when you use the tongue scan feature. This is not Face ID, TrueDepth identity data, or biometric enrollment templates created by iOS for device authentication.

What we collect: A tongue photo per scan you initiate, encoded for transport as JPEG (typically resized on-device before upload).

How we collect it: Only after you start a scan, capture or select a photo, review the in-app disclosure, and choose Allow and Continue. The App does not upload photos in the background.

How we use it: We use the photo only to provide the AI-generated visual observation you request and to display the resulting history on your device. We do not use it for advertising or identity recognition.

Third-party recipient: The image is sent over HTTPS to servers we operate, then forwarded to Google LLC's Gemini API for automated visual processing. Google's processing is governed by the Gemini API terms and applicable data-processing terms. We require service providers that receive personal data to provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy and required by applicable law and Apple's guidelines. We do not sell tongue photos or share them with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention: On-device history and locally saved tongue photos remain until you delete app data (Settings → Delete My Data), uninstall the App, or erase the device. Our processing endpoints do not intentionally retain uploaded images and return responses with no-store caching instructions; temporary operational data is removed through scheduled cleanup. Google may retain limited data for service operation, abuse prevention, security, or legal requirements under its applicable API terms.

Consent and withdrawal: You can withdraw permission at any time by turning off Allow photo sharing in Settings → Share Data with AI. After withdrawal, the App blocks further AI photo uploads unless you grant permission again. Withdrawal does not remove local history; use Settings → Delete My Data for local deletion.

AI Analysis and Automated Outputs

The photo observation feature uses Google LLC's Gemini API through our backend to describe visible features such as color, coating, surface appearance, and shape. The App does not send your profile or onboarding answers with the photo.

Outputs may be imperfect, incomplete, or incorrect and can be affected by lighting, angle, food, drink, and time of day. They are not health measurements and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
