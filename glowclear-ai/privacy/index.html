Facial Photographs (Face Data)

This section describes "face data" as Apple uses the term: still photographs you choose to capture or import that show your face when you use Face Scan. This is not Face ID, TrueDepth identity data, or biometric enrollment templates created by iOS for device authentication.

What we collect: A single face photo (or a photo from your library) per scan you initiate, encoded for transport as JPEG.

How we use it: Solely to generate in-app skin-related wellness insights (scores, labels, summaries, and similar outputs), to show scan history and trends on your device, and to operate, secure, and debug the analysis pipeline.

Third parties and storage: The image is sent over HTTPS to servers we operate, then forwarded to Google LLC's Gemini API for automated vision analysis. Google processes the image only to return the analysis output to our service; Google's processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy documentation applicable to API use. We do not sell face photos and do not share them with data brokers.

Retention: On-device thumbnails, scores, and related history remain until you delete app data (Settings → Delete My Data), uninstall the App, or erase the device. On our servers, the uploaded image is deleted from our primary processing database once analysis completes. All face data is fully removed within 1 month. Backups or residual logs may briefly retain fragments consistent with our infrastructure practices.
